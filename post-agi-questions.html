Questions for a post-AGI world

Economics

  • Does the stock market become a pure bet on energy and compute, or does something stranger happen?
  • What happens to interest rates if economic growth accelerates 10x?
  • Is "I made this myself" the new luxury good?
  • Do small nations with cheap energy (Iceland, Norway, Paraguay) suddenly matter a lot more?
  • Is infinite intelligence the new resource trap?
  • Should AIs pay income tax?
  • How does the Fed track economic activity when the majority of transactions are agent to agent or within one data centre?

Culture

  • Does religion grow or shrink when the most articulate voices on either side are artificial?
  • If an AI system is better than you at introspection, does it know you better than you know yourself?
  • How long until the most interesting entity you talk to in a day isn't human?
